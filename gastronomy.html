Köttbullar (Swedish Meatballs)
Swedish meatballs may be Sweden's most iconic dish thanks to IKEA, which put Sweden on the World Map. There are actually several ways in which meatballs can be served, but the most common is with mashed potatoes, cream sauce, and cranberries.		 Skagen Toast
Another one of my favorites, and also that of other Swedes, is the Skagen toast, a toast with a mix of shrimp, mayonnaise, and sour cream. Smörgåstårta (sandwich cake)
It literally means sandwich cake, and when you see one, you'll know how much we love bread and sandwiches. There are many different types of Smörgåstårta, but they are all made in layers with various toppings and ingredients.

Surströmming
If you want to have a unique culinary experience when you visit Sweden, then Surströmming is what you're looking for. The smell is unbearable, but it is quite a classic. If it sticks to your clothes, or even your skin, the smell will linger for some time.

You can buy a can of Surströmming at any supermarket and I promise it will be memorable. I recommend that you record the experience so you can capture your first reaction for life.
